Hi, I'm Constantin! I'm a Partner Solutions Engineer at numo, and before that I did the same job at Google.

My job is building bridges. I work side by side with partners to design, build and launch integrations, translating between what a partner needs and what the platform can do, and sweating the details until it just works.

Earlier in my career I spent years shipping native Android and iOS apps and full-stack web apps. I've been the developer on the other side of the API, so I know where the rough edges are and how to get past them.
